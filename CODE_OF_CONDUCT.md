# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **XENOPHORA** community a harassment-free, respectful, and intellectually generous experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or discipline across mathematics, graphics programming, artificial life, and generative art.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating curiosity, artistic rigor, and constructive technical critique
- Giving clear, reproducible shader/math feedback and welcoming newcomers
- Respecting differing aesthetic and engineering perspectives
- Giving attribution to mathematical papers, artists, and open-source contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without consent
- Disrupting issues, discussions, or pull requests in bad faith

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainer at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
